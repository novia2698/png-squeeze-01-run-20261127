Builder brief — make.fun bounty png-squeeze-01-run-20261127: "Squeeze screenshots into the smallest PNG, 27 November 2026".

The bounty: write one program, main.ts at the repository root (Node 22, no dependencies), that rewrites eight 1280x720 RGBA screenshots losslessly as PNGs in as few bytes as possible. Each instance lives under /input/<id>/image.png and the answer goes to /output/<id>/image.png, at most 4 MiB each. The whole set must finish inside one 60-second wall budget, run twice byte-identically (deterministic: choose by byte size, never by the clock), with no network and no clock reads.

Reward: $100 total over 50 paid places — 1st to 10th get $4, 11th to 20th get $3, 21st to 50th get $1. Entries close 29 November 2026 at 05:00 UTC; results are due 20 December 2026. A paid place needs every scored instance answered and a score strictly above the house baseline, make.fun's own fixed-paeth 1.0.0 — so the goal is not just a complete entry but one that beats that baseline by a clear margin and lands inside the paid places. Aim at the paid range: the practice set already shows 705,452 against the baseline's 420,399, so keep that gap, don't trade it away for cleverness.

What the entry builds: a single-file lossless PNG recompressor. For each screenshot it chooses the cheapest exact form (palette when there are at most 256 colours, grey or grey+alpha when every pixel is grey, RGB when every pixel is opaque, RGBA otherwise, with a tRNS key for the one-transparent-colour case), searches row filters (fixed filters, signed-sum and byte-pair heuristics, plus brute force scored by a real deflate of the row in context), and re-encodes the winner at zlib level 9 under several strategies, keeping the smallest stream. Every choice is decided by measured byte size, first wins on ties, so two runs produce identical bytes.
